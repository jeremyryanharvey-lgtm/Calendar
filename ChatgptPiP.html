ChatgptPiP

Live screen capture → Picture-in-Picture experiment

This experiment asks Safari to share your screen, displays that live stream in a video, then tries to put the video into PiP. If supported, you can switch to ChatGPT while the floating video remains visible.

Checking this Safari version…

Screen sharing is controlled by iOS. You must approve it in the system prompt. This page cannot secretly capture your screen.

If screen capture is unavailable, this page will say so rather than showing a pretend feed. The previous test already established that ordinary video PiP works on your phone; this test targets the missing live-capture step.
